# Bazaar · the wardrobe index

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. Source: the user's commercial audit brief, the actual four-category inventory and [Scroll Craft](https://github.com/nateherkai/scroll-craft). Adapted to an existing transactional React storefront.

- Intent: “Classic but modern”, “Premium but not flashy”, “Minimal but not empty”. Independent fashion retail from Surat; no invented brand history, supplier endorsements or guarantees.
- Grammar: a short, browsable wardrobe index. Recognition (ink masthead), curiosity (choose a garment family), confidence (its real photo, maker and price), discovery (new arrivals), return (newsletter).
- Signature: turn the category index beside a photographic plate. The plate, product caption and category destination agree. The buyer can open the exact piece or continue into its category. No timed carousel.
- Peak: selecting a category and immediately seeing a real, purchasable piece. The page identifies the product rather than treating the photo as decoration. Desktop uses facing pages; phone places choices and caption around a shorter plate.
- Quiet close: one working newsletter form. Assets on the site continue to come from product records. Local research photos are independent. No generated campaign imagery, rigid scroll capture, video payload or new motion library.
- Motion: CSS view-timeline depth on the photograph where supported; text and controls stay fixed relative to their panel. The static composition works with reduced motion, touch and older browsers.
