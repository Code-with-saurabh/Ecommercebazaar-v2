## Brand & Style
This design system defines a luxury European fashion digital flagship, marrying classic print editorial prestige with uncompromising modern commerce precision. Built for discerning buyers, curators, and collectors, the aesthetic evokes the tactility of heavy-stock independent art volumes, architectural minimalism, and gallery-level cataloguing.

The design movement synthesizes **High-End Modern Editorial** with **Disciplined Continental Functionalism**:
- Unapologetic spatial breathing room that commands authority and gives garments museum-grade presence.
- Strict typographic discipline, contrasting romantic, razor-sharp serif display titles with cold, rational, ultra-legible grotesque sans micro-data.
- Non-ornamental surface architecture: depth is expressed through crisp hairline partitions, architectural asymmetry, and subtle tonal shifts rather than volumetric drop shadows.
- Dynamic tension between classical restraint (warm limestone, ink, crisp white) and deliberate avant-garde signatures (the warm vermilion-to-chartreuse identity gradient).

## Colors
The palette evokes archival rag paper, rich bespoke printing inks, and purposeful mineral accents:

- **Atmosphere & Surfaces:**
  - Canvas Base: `#F8F7F4` (Warm limestone off-white), insulating the eye from stark digital glare.
  - Surface Elevation: `#FFFFFF` (Pure optic white), strictly reserved for product stage cards, floating panels, dropdown sheets, and editorial image backdrops.
  - Hairline Rule & Dividers: `#E5E4DE` (Muted bone rule), creating subtle 1px compositional grids.
  - Sub-surface / Inactive Fill: `#EEEEEC`.

- **Ink & Typography:**
  - Primary Typographic Ink: `#1E1E1E` (Warm charcoal black). Used for headline editorial type, primary structural icons, and active selection perimeters.
  - Secondary Meta / Captions: `#4A4A48` (Slate charcoal), ensuring effortless readability while sitting a step behind primary text.
  - Tertiary / Ghost Ink: `#767571` (Subtle graphite) for specifications, SKU numbers, and contextual labels.

- **Focal Accents & Semantics:**
  - Primary Accent (`#088178` - Deep Sea Teal): The singular focus driver. Used sparingly for key conversion triggers ("Add to Cart", "Instant Purchase"), live editorial badges, and interactive focal points.
  - Tertiary Accent (`#EF233C` - Signal Crimson): Exclusively utilized for markdown tags, urgent limited-edition inventory alerts, and error states.
  - Signature Brand Gradient: A linear sequence from `#FF6B6B` (Warm Coral Vermilion) to `#CACA2B` (Acid Chartreuse), applied strictly to the wordmark signature, bespoke hero editorial banners, and VIP membership accents.

## Typography
Typographic treatment balances literary sophistication with rigorous operational metadata:

- **Playfair Display (Serif Display & Headings):** Conveys heritage, high craftsmanship, and deliberate pacing. Used with natural, proportional line heights and restrained sizing to avoid gaudiness. When styling section titles, opt for regular or medium weights; avoid heavy synthetic bolds.
- **Manrope (Geometric Modern Sans):** Drives functional navigation, pricing tables, product details, long-form editorial journalism, and interactive components. It offers exceptional tabular alignment for numerical data.
- **Micro-Typographic Standard:** All uppercase labels (`label-caps`) must feature an expansive letter spacing (+0.12em) to evoke high-end perfume packaging and atelier catalog tags. Prices never take serif styling—they remain grounded in clean, precise Manrope to preserve transaction clarity.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a fixed-bleed hybrid architecture capped at a maximum width of `1320px` to guarantee editorial integrity on ultra-wide screens.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column asymmetric or rational layout with `2rem` (32px) gutters and generous `3rem` (48px) safe margins. Editorial spreads alternate between 6-6 column split heroes, 8-4 catalog stories, and rhythmic 3-3-3-3 item showcases.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.25rem` (20px) outer margins.
- **Vertical Editorial Cadence:** Space is treated as a physical material. Sections are distanced by rhythmic intervals (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`). Text blocks next to full-bleed photography never span edge-to-edge; they remain clamped to restrained line-lengths (max 60–65 characters).

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with continental editorial discipline, depth is achieved without heavy, murky drop shadows:

- **Surface Layering:** Visual hierarchy is created via juxtaposed surface tones: the primary canvas sits at `#F8F7F4`, while active cards, panels, and floating drawers sit on crisp `#FFFFFF`.
- **Architectural Hairlines:** Layer boundaries rely on a razor-sharp 1px border (`#E5E4DE`). Rather than hovering over the page, elements lock into structural matrices.
- **Ambient Shadow (Contextual Floating Elements Only):** Modals, persistent bottom action bars, and mega-menus utilize a barely perceptible, whisper-quiet diffuse occlusion shadow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.05), 0 2px 6px 0 rgba(30, 30, 30, 0.02);`
- **Zero Elevation on In-Flow Cards:** Product cards, lookbook plates, and editorial blocks have `box-shadow: none` at all times, relying exclusively on imagery edge-contrast and perimeter hairlines.

## Shapes
The design system implements a tailored, architectural silhouette. Radii are strictly kept between 0px and 4px to emulate precision-cut Italian cardboard, textile hangtags, and crisp stationery:

- Standard interactives (buttons, inputs, status tags): `2px` to `4px` (`roundedness: 1`).
- Pill shapes, rounded bubble cards, and circular chips are strictly forbidden.
- Editorial image containers retain sharp right angles (`0px` border-radius) or a maximum of `2px` to maintain the photographic framing of a printed fashion journal.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion Button:** Filled with deep sea teal (`#088178`), text in `#FFFFFF` (Manrope Medium, `label-md`), 2px border radius. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover state: deepens to `#066760`. Zero shadow.
- **Secondary / Action Button:** Solid `#1E1E1E` background with white text, or clean `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#1E1E1E` border and ink text. Padding: `14px 28px`.
- **Editorial Text Button:** Ghost background, underline offset 6px with a 1px rule in `#1E1E1E`, accompanied by a minimal right hairline arrow. Hover state transitions line opacity and tracking.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Card:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background or transparent on `#F8F7F4`. Clean 3:4 aspect ratio imagery with razor 1px borders (`#E5E4DE`) on hovering state or quiet borderless layouts.
- **Card Metadata:** Designer label in `label-caps` (`#767571`), title in `headline-sm` or `title-editorial`, and numeric price in `price-focal` (`#1E1E1E` or accented in `#088178`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Category Chips:** Rectangular, 2px radius, `#FFFFFF` background with 1px solid `#E5E4DE` border. Padding: `6px 14px`. Text styled in `label-caps`.
- **Sale & Archive Badges:** Crisp rectangular tags in `#EF233C` with stark `#FFFFFF` type (`label-caps`), positioned flush against image corners.

### Form Inputs & Search
- **Input Fields:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px solid `#E5E4DE` border, 2px radius. Height: `48px`. Padding: `0 16px`. Active/focus state switches border to 1px `#1E1E1E` with zero glowing halos.
- **Editorial Search Bar:** Clean, open underlined bottom border (1px `#1E1E1E`), transparent background, with Playfair Display placeholder text.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** 16px square, 2px radius, 1px border (`#1E1E1E`). Checked state fills with `#1E1E1E` housing a razor-fine white vector check.
- **Size Selectors:** Square tiles (`44px x 44px`), 2px radius, centered `label-md` type. Out-of-stock items feature a subtle 45-degree diagonal hairline slash across the tile.

### Curatorial Accordions & Specification Lists
- Ultra-thin horizontal borders (`1px solid #E5E4DE`) separating composition details, provenance, and sizing charts. Subtle minimalist `+` and `−` hairline icons for expansion toggles.
